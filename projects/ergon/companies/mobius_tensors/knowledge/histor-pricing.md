# Histor pricing (INTERNAL)

INTERNAL: not for clients or partners yet. Use freely inside the team; put prices in a
customer-facing draft only when the user asks for a quote or estimate. Decided 2026-10-13.

## Software (one-time, per site)

- Site with up to 8 cameras: $6,000.
- Each extra camera above 8: $400.

## Yearly subscription (updates, fixes, support)

- $15 per camera per month, billed yearly ($180 per camera per year).
- Minimum $600 a year per site (applies up to 3 cameras).
- In a pitch: "$15 per camera per month", or "less than 50 cents per camera per day".

| Cameras | Software | Yearly |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 2 | $6,000 | $600 (minimum) |
| 4 | $6,000 | $720 |
| 8 | $6,000 | $1,440 |
| 12 | $7,600 | $2,160 |
| 16 | $9,200 | $2,880 |
| 32 | $15,600 | $5,760 |

Formula: software = $6,000 + $400 x max(0, cameras - 8); yearly = max($600, $180 x cameras).

## Everything is included

The site price includes every Histor module: memory and plain-language search, areas and lines,
crowds, bags left behind, wrong way, plate reading, person down and activity, fire and smoke,
sound alerts, face search (with written approval), camera health, alert checking, dashboard and
Telegram. There are no paid modules.

## Extra modules (only if needed)

A detection Histor does not have yet: $5,000 per extra module, built for the site, one-time, no
yearly fee.

## Hardware (at cost plus a small margin)

Every quote includes hardware sized from the camera count; remove what the customer already has
(often the cameras). These prices are PLACEHOLDERS until real supplier prices are in.

| Item | Price | How many |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Server (CPU) | $1,800 | 1 per 8 cameras |
| PoE switch, 16 ports | $300 | 1 per 15 cameras |
| IP camera | $150 | 1 per camera |
| NVIDIA GPU (optional) | $700 | only when needed |

Example, a full 8-camera system: $6,000 software + $3,300 hardware (1 server, 1 switch, 8
cameras) = $9,300, then $1,440 a year.

## Partner commissions

On software and extra modules only, never on hardware or the yearly subscription: Silver 12%,
Gold 25%, Platinum buys at 40% off. Example, 8 cameras ($6,000): Silver $720, Gold $1,500,
Platinum buys at $3,600.
