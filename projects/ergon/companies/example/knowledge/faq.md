# Example Co. customer questions

A sample document for the made-up Example Co.

## Returns

Unused items can be returned within 30 days for a full refund. Assembled desks can be returned
within 14 days; the assembly fee is not refunded.

## Warranty

Desks have a 5-year warranty on the frame and 2 years on the motor. Chairs have a 3-year
warranty.

## Delivery times

Items in stock are delivered within 3 working days. Made-to-order desks take 2 to 3 weeks.
