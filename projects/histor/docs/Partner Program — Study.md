# Partner Program

*Histor is a Mobius Tensors product. Draft v2, 2026-10-13: being rethought, nothing here is final.*

## The idea

People who bring us a new customer (for Histor or any other Mobius Tensors solution) earn a commission. We only pay when the customer pays us.

Public wording until this is decided (website and LinkedIn): *"Earn a commission on every customer you bring. Contact us for details."* No rates are published.

## Two kinds of payment

Every customer pays us two kinds of payment:

| Payment | What it is | Commission? |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **System purchase** | The one-time payment at the start. The customer buys the system and keeps it: the hardware (cameras if needed, server, switch) and the AI software, installed and set up | **Yes, on the AI software part only.** Hardware is passed through at cost plus a small margin, so it earns no commission |
| **Yearly subscription** | Paid every year: updates, fixes and support | **No**: never any commission or discount on it |

Why: the yearly subscription pays for the work that keeps each customer running. Keeping it free of commission keeps it predictable, and nobody earns forever from a single introduction.

## The three levels

| | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Anyone: friends, consultants, freelancers | Technically qualified people: AI engineers, data scientists, IT professionals, university professors | CCTV and IT installation companies |
| What they do | Introduce the customer | Introduce, help scope the solution and help close the deal | Resell, install and give first-line support |
| What they get | **12%** of the AI software part of the system purchase | **25%** of the AI software part | **40% off** the AI software part, which they resell |
| How they join | Sign the partner agreement **before** introducing anyone | Signed directly as Gold: their qualifications are enough, no deals to prove first. We offer a short product session so they can explain the system | A CCTV/IT company + a signed reseller agreement + our installation training before their first install |

All rates apply to the AI software part of the system purchase only: never to hardware, never to the yearly subscription. Rates are draft until the price list confirms they fit our costs.

### Platinum details

- The installer gives first-line support; we handle the software (second line). The yearly subscription stays ours, with no discount.
- Area set in the agreement, not exclusive unless agreed, so two installers don't compete for the same city.
- The installer can supply its own hardware (it earns its own margin there) or buy it through us.

## Other solutions (not Histor)

For our services (AI agents and chatbots, agent rollout, automations, training, forecasting) most of the cost is our own time. The commission on the first contract is lower than for Histor and capped: **[S]% of the first contract, up to [cap]** (to decide).

## The rules

1. **Agreement first.** Only people who signed a partner agreement are partners. An introduction made before signing earns nothing, even if the customer buys later.
2. The partner registers the customer with us **before** we contact them. First one to register wins.
3. The customer must buy within 6 months of registration.
4. We pay the partner within 30 days after the customer pays the system purchase.
5. If the customer cancels within 90 days, the commission is taken back.
6. Partners cannot promise prices or features. Only we send quotes.
7. Everything is written in the agreement each partner signs (have a lawyer check it, including how we pay: USD, bank transfer, partner invoice).

## What partners get besides money

- **All:** brochures, a one-page Histor pitch, a short demo video.
- **Gold:** a live Histor demo and joint meetings with customers.
- **Platinum:** a demo kit at cost, co-branded material, priority support, and the leads we receive in their area.

## The price list

- The Histor price list (internal) is in [Pricing — Internal](<Pricing — Internal.md>): **$6,000 per site for up to 8 cameras, $400 per extra camera**; the yearly subscription is $15 per camera per month, billed yearly, at least $600 a year. Every Histor module is part of the system; only an extra module built for a customer costs more ($5,000).
- Example: on an 8-camera site ($6,000 of software), Silver earns $720, Gold $1,500, and a Platinum installer buys it for $3,600 and resells it at $6,000. On a 16-camera site ($9,200): $1,104, $2,300, and buys at $5,520.
- Extra modules are software, so they earn commission too. Hardware and the yearly subscription never do.
- Check the rates against our costs now that the price list exists.
- Track everything in one simple spreadsheet: partner, customer, level for that deal, AI software amount, commission, paid date.

## What changed from v1 (2026-10-10)

- Commission only on the AI software part of the system purchase, never on hardware or the yearly subscription (v1: a share of the first year, plus 5% of the second year for Gold and 25% off the licence every year for Platinum).
- Deal-count conditions removed (3 deals in 12 months for Gold; 6 deals in 12 months for Platinum). Gold is signed directly; Platinum needs a company, a reseller agreement and our installation training.
- Draft rates: Silver 12%, Gold 25%, Platinum 40% off (v1: 10% / 15% + 5% / 30% off setup + 25% off the licence).
- Rates removed from the website and LinkedIn until the price list exists.
- Separate, capped commission for services.
