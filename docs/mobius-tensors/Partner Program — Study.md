# Mobius Tensors Partner Program

Oct 5, 2026 · Rachad Lakkis

## The idea

People who bring us a new customer (for Argus or any other solution) get a percentage of what that customer pays. We only pay when the customer pays us.

## The three levels

| | Silver | Gold | Platinum |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Who | Anyone: friends, consultants, freelancers | People who also help sell | CCTV installers and integrators |
| What they do | Introduce the customer | Introduce and help close the deal | Sell, install and support the customer |
| What they get | 10% of the first year | 15% of the first year + 5% of the second year | 30% off setup and 25% off the license, every year |
| How to get there | Sign the agreement | 3 deals in 12 months | 6 deals in 12 months, or pass our installation training |

Hardware (cameras, server, switch) earns no commission.

These rates are normal in the market: introducers usually get 10–20% and resellers 20–40% ([DealHub](https://dealhub.io/glossary/partner-margin/), [Track360](https://track360.io/blog/saas-affiliate-commission-rates-benchmark-2026)).

## Example: one Argus site with 8 cameras

Assumed price (we have no price list yet): $3,000 setup + $3,840 license a year. That makes $14,520 over 3 years.

| Level | Partner gets over 3 years | We keep |
| --- | --- | --- |
| No partner | $0 | $14,520 |
| Silver | $684 | $13,836 |
| Gold | $1,218 | $13,302 |
| Platinum | $3,780 | $10,740 (but the partner does the installation and support) |

## The rules

1. The partner tells us about the customer **before** we contact them. First one to tell us wins.
2. The customer must buy within 6 months.
3. We pay the partner within 30 days after the customer pays us.
4. If the customer cancels within 90 days, the commission is taken back.
5. Partners cannot promise prices or features. Only we send quotes.
6. Everything is written in a short agreement that each partner signs.

## What partners get besides money

- **All:** brochures, a one-page Argus pitch, a short demo video.
- **Gold:** a live Argus demo and joint meetings with customers.
- **Platinum:** a demo kit at cost, co-branded material, priority support, and the leads we receive in their area.

## How to start

1. **Month 1:** set the Argus price list, write the agreement (have a lawyer check it), prepare the partner kit.
2. **Months 2–4:** try it with 5–10 people we know and 1–2 CCTV installers.
3. **Months 5–6:** if at least one deal closed, open it to everyone.
4. **Month 12:** look at the results and adjust the rates.

Track everything in one simple spreadsheet: partner, customer, deal value, commission, paid date.
